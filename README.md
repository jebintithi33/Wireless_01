**5G Technology and the Future of Telecommunications**

This presentation explores the evolution of cellular networks from 1G to 5G, focusing on how 5G technology is revolutionizing global communication, industry, and everyday life.
It highlights the key differences between 4G (LTE) and 5G, discusses their real-world applications, business benefits, and challenges, and looks ahead to the future trends shaping modern telecommunications.

**Topics Covered**

1.Introduction to Cellular Networks

2.Evolution of Cellular Networks (1G to 5G)

3.What is 4G (LTE)?

4.What is 5G Technology?

5.4G vs 5G: Real-Life Impact

6.Why 5G is Better Than 4G

7.Benefits of 5G for Businesses

8.Challenges in 5G Implementation

9.Trends and the Future of Telecommunications

**Objectives**

1.Understand the concept and evolution of cellular network technologies.

2.Learn how 5G improves speed, latency, and connectivity.

3.Identify the practical impact of 5G in various industries.

4.Explore the opportunities and challenges businesses face with 5G deployment.

5.Discuss future trends shaping global telecommunications.
